# Example: 8 × 10 m yard in Absheron, with kids

Fictional user. Garden part only, shortened for a phone.

## Request

> Our yard in Buzovna is 8 × 10 m, south of the house, full sun, windy, water from a tank. Two small kids and a dog. We want a shady place to sit, a play corner and a few vegetables. Budget 2500 AZN.

## Result

**1. Summary** — 80 m², hot, dry, windy, sandy soil. Pergola terrace by the house, play area in view, vegetables in the far sunny end, gravel instead of lawn.

**2. Plan** — N↑ (top = house side), scale: 1 char = 50 cm, 1 line = 1 m
```
+-----HOUSE------+
|TTTTTTTT..UUU   |
|TTTTTTTT..      |
|TTTTTTTT..      |
|        ..  PPPP|
|GGGG    ..  PPPP|
|GGGG    ..  PPPP|
|VVVVVV  ..      |
|VVVVVV  ..      |
|VVVVVV  ..   ooo|
|        ..   ooo|
+--------DD------+
```
`T` terrace 4 × 3 m under a grapevine pergola · `U` bins, tap, tank 1.5 × 1 m · `P` play 2 × 3 m (soft bark) · `G` lavender bed 2 × 2 m · `V` vegetables 3 × 3 m · `o` shrubs · `.` gravel path 1 m · `DD` gate.

**3. Plants**
| Plant | Sun / water | Size (H × W) | Kids/pets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grapevine (Vitis vinifera), pergola | sun / medium | climber | ✅ |
| Lavender (Lavandula angustifolia) | sun / low | 0.5 × 0.6 m | ✅ |
| Rosemary (Salvia rosmarinus) | sun / low | 1 × 1 m | ✅ |
| Pomegranate (Punica granatum) | sun / low | 3–5 × 3 m | ✅ |
| Tamarisk (Tamarix), windbreak | sun / low | 3–5 × 3 m | ✅ as far as known |
| ~~Oleander~~ | — | — | ⚠ toxic to children and pets — left out |

**4. Quantities**
- Gravel path: 10 × 1 × 0.06 m = 0.6 m³ → buy 0.7 m³ ≈ 1.1 t
- Lavender: 4 m² ÷ (0.6 × 0.6) = 11 plants
- Play-area bark, 10 cm: 6 m² × 0.1 = 0.6 m³

**5. Water**
- Vegetables: 9 m² × 30 L = 270 L a week in summer, in 2–3 waterings
- Drip: tomato 4 L ÷ 2 L/h emitter × 60 = 120 min per watering
- Sandy soil: add 5–10 cm compost to the vegetable bed; mulch everything 5–8 cm

**6. Sowing (approx., Absheron)** — tomato/pepper seeds indoors mid Feb – mid Mar; plant out mid Apr – early May; greens in Mar and Sep–Oct. Ask a local nursery for this year's timing.

**7. Budget (approx. AZN)** — Phase 1: path, tap, drip kit 600–800 · Phase 2: pergola + vine, shrubs 900–1200 · Phase 3: lavender, play bark, solar lights 300–450 · contingency 10–15%.

Safety: wired outdoor lights or a pump → licensed electrician. A pergola is fine on its own posts; fixing it to the house wall → ask a builder.
