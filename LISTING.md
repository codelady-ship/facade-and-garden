## Summary (≤ 300)

```text
Free facade and small-garden planner. Facade and colour ideas with hex codes, an ASCII yard plan, plant tables with toxicity flags, path and step sizes, gravel/paver/plant quantities, watering and drip times, a sowing calendar, pest first aid and a phased budget. Risky work goes to pros.
```

## Description
```markdown
Describe your house, yard or balcony, which way it faces, your climate, budget and how you use the space. You get a plan that fits your weather and your wallet, in your language and short enough for a phone.

You get:
- 3 facade ideas with material pairs, care notes and colour schemes in hex
- garden zones on an ASCII plan (sitting, play, growing, utility)
- plant tables with Latin names, mature sizes and a kids/pets safety column
- path, terrace and step sizes, plus gravel, paver, mulch and plant counts
- weekly water needs and drip run times; a low-water garden for dry climates
- a vegetable sowing calendar, companion planting and pest first aid
- a balcony container garden, a phased budget and a care calendar
- an image prompt for a before/after concept

Knows Azerbaijani climates (Absheron, Lankaran, Ganja, Guba) and works for any city.

**Where it stops:** retaining walls, structure, outdoor wiring, gas, drainage near foundations and permits go to licensed pros and the local authority, with no how-to steps. Dates and prices are approximate.
```

## Limitations / FAQ
- Sowing dates are approximate (no official frost dates were found for Azerbaijani regions); it says to ask a local nursery.
- Toxicity flags come from the ASPCA pet list; it is not medical advice.
- Azerbaijani rules: house notification (Code Art. 80), no-permit small works (Art. 79.1, amended 2026), İçərişəhər consent. It always adds `verify locally`.
