# Paths, terraces and steps (quick reference)

Common good-practice values, rounded. Local rules and a builder have the final word.

## Paths

| Path | Width |
|---|---|
| Main path, two people side by side or wheelchair | 120 cm (min 90) |
| Single-person path | 80–90 cm |
| Stepping stones (centre to centre) | 60–65 cm apart, stones 40–50 cm |
| Path with a wheelbarrow | 90 cm+ |
| Car strip / driveway | 250–300 cm |

- Cross fall (sideways slope) about 1–2% so rain runs off; always away from the house.
- Gravel paths need an edge (metal, brick or timber) and a weed membrane.
- A path should lead somewhere useful, by the shortest natural line; people cut corners otherwise.

## Terrace / patio

| Use | Minimum size |
|---|---|
| Two chairs and a small table | 2.0 × 2.0 m |
| Table for 4 with chairs pulled out | 3.0 × 3.0 m |
| Table for 6 | 3.5 × 4.0 m |
| Plus a walkway past the table | add 0.9 m |
| BBQ area (keep away from walls, trees and fences) | 1.5 × 1.5 m extra |

- Fall 1–2% away from the house (1–2 cm per metre).
- Keep the terrace at least 15 cm below the damp-proof course or indoor floor level, or ask a builder.
- Shade: pergola 2.4–2.7 m high; a deciduous tree or vine (e.g. grapevine) gives summer shade and winter sun.

## Steps (outdoor)

Comfort check (Blondel): **2 × rise + tread ≈ 60–65 cm**.

| | Indoor typical | Outdoor comfortable |
|---|---|---|
| Rise (height) | 15–18 cm | 12–16 cm |
| Tread (depth) | 26–30 cm | 30–40 cm |

Example: rise 15 cm → tread 60 − 30 = 30 to 65 − 30 = 35 cm. Choose 33 cm.

- All rises in one flight must be equal; a different step trips people.
- Minimum width 90 cm, 120 cm for a main entrance.
- Handrail: from about 3 steps, or where the side drop is 50–60 cm or more (railing needed).
- Ramps: 1:12 or gentler (8%), landings every 9 m, 150 cm landing at the top and bottom.
- Light every step at night, and use non-slip surfaces (no polished stone outdoors).

## Fences, edges and raised beds

- Raised beds: 30–80 cm high, max 120 cm wide (reach the middle from both sides), 60–80 cm if against a wall.
- Low walls up to about 60 cm can be a DIY garden job if they hold nothing back. Anything retaining soil, or higher, is engineer work.
- Fences on the street line or shared boundary: check local rules and talk to the neighbour first.

## Drainage basics

- Water must never flow toward the house or into the neighbour's plot.
- Gutters and downpipes lead into a rain tank, soakaway or drain at least 1–2 m from foundations (a builder sizes it).
- Clay soils hold water: plant on slight mounds; sandy Absheron soils drain fast: add compost and mulch.
